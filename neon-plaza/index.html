NEON PLAZA

3-Page Overview — Internal

ELEVATOR PITCH: AKANE’s tempo-driven flow meets Zero Sievert’s extraction tension — set in 1988 neon NYC.

Chain kills into combo-accelerating music, manage escalating HEAT, outsmart a roaming LIEUTENANT, kill the KINGPIN, and extract with rare tech.

The Player Fantasy

  • Be the city’s loudest ghost: lethal top-down combat where momentum is everything.
  • Choose your pace: aggro players go loud, stealth players go quiet, smart players MacGyver.
  • Steal corruption’s trophies: rare tech, signature weapons, and hideout-specific collectibles.
  • Escape under pressure: alarms, sirens, patrols, and a Lieutenant hunting your footsteps.

Core Loop (20–30 Minutes)

  1. Infiltrate a procedural hideout (floor-by-floor).
  2. Loot + MacGyver (keys, locks, hacking, gadgets, shortcuts).
  3. Build Combo by chaining kills → music intensifies → loot quantity spikes.
  4. Heat Escalates as you act → security changes + elite units appear.
  5. The Lieutenant Hunts (optional mini-boss with high-tier reward + Heat relief).
  6. Kill the Kingpin in a handcrafted arena.
  7. Extract at one of two exits (each with different risks + mechanics).

Heat + Lieutenant: The Signature Tension

  • HEAT is your pressure gauge: rising Heat triggers escalating security responses and higher stakes.
  • LIEUTENANT is the hideout’s roaming predator: tracks by sound and sight, punishes sloppy runs.
  • CRITICAL RULE: If the Lieutenant is alive and HEAT > 50%, they can join the final fight / extraction chaos alongside the Kingpin.
  • Result: optional hunt becomes a strategic decision — remove the threat now, or risk a nightmare ending.

Modes

REVENGE MODE (Primary)

  • Extraction roguelite run structure.
  • Persistent progression + unlocks.
  • Death loses run loot, but long-term progress remains.
  • Playstyle freedom: loud / stealth / smart are all viable.

ORIGIN MODE (Secondary)

  • Linear boss hunt (training + narrative).
  • Introduces gangs, corruption themes, and your revenge motive.
  • Curated challenges teach loadouts + systems.

Phase Scope

PHASE 1 (12 Months): Base game launches with 2 hideouts — Office Tower + Hotel.

PHASE 2 (+12 Months): Expansion adds 2 hideouts — Shopping Center + Casino.

The “Trailer Moment”

  • Combo crescendo: the soundtrack hits peak intensity during a perfect streak.
  • Boss kill → sprint to extract: Heat maxed out, rare tech in hand, alarms screaming.
  • Lieutenant vengeance: you spared them, Heat rose, and now they’re in the final chaos.

Page 2 — Tech + Systems

Design Pillars → Implementation Targets

  • Readability at speed: lethal combat needs clean silhouettes, instant feedback, strong audio cues.
  • Pressure with counterplay: Heat rises, but stealth + gadgets + hacks let players re-shape the situation.
  • Music as state feedback: players hear danger + flow without checking UI.
  • Procedural, not random: rules + quality gates prevent “bad runs.”

System Stack (The “Neon Plaza Engine”)

System What It Does Why It Matters
Music State Machine Stealth → Combat → Combo → Extraction (bar-synced transitions where possible). Makes momentum audible. Defines the identity.
Combo Chaining kills increases intensity + loot quantity. Reinforces “flow-state” play and creates shareable peaks.
Heat Escalation tiers that change patrols, threats, and events. Turns every run into a push-your-luck story.
Lieutenant (Hunter) Roaming predator with counterplay + big rewards. Joins endgame if spared at high Heat. Run-defining drama + meaningful strategic choice.
Procedural Hideouts Chunk templates + rule assembly + quality gates + handcrafted boss arenas. Replayability without unfair layouts.
MacGyver Toolkit Locks/keys/hacks/gadgets create alternate routes and solutions. Supports stealth and “smart” playstyles.

Lieutenant + Kingpin Integration (Narrative + Gameplay)

Rule: each hideout has a Kingpin + Lieutenant who represent the same corruption theme — but from different angles.

Gameplay Role Split:

  • Lieutenant: roaming, reactive, punishing — tests adaptation and stealth under pressure.
  • Kingpin: setpiece fight — tests mastery, loadout prep, and execution under high Heat.
  • Merge Condition: Lieutenant alive + Heat > 50% → “two-boss chaos” at the end.

Procedural Hideouts (Rules + Quality Gates)

  • Chunk Templates: curated rooms with purpose (combat pocket, stealth corridor, loot room, lock/hack room).
  • Pacing Rules: quiet → spike → recovery (no endless hallway slog).
  • Key Rules: never lock progression behind a single failure point (multiple solutions: hack, key, brute tool).
  • Quality Gates: protect readability (sightlines, cover, enemy spawn fairness, escape routes).
  • Handcrafted Endcaps: boss arenas + signature setpieces per hideout.

Simulated Verticality (Top-Down Across Floors)

OFFICE TOWER

  • Traversal: vents + stairwells + security shafts.
  • Risk: tight corridors, cameras, locked shortcuts.
  • Feel: corporate labyrinth — clean, cold, deadly.

HOTEL

  • Traversal: elevators + service corridors.
  • Risk: open suites, long sightlines, ambush routes.
  • Feel: luxury maze — secrets behind every door.

MacGyver Toolkit (Locks, Hacks, Gadgets)

  • Locks & Keys: keycards, coded locks, master keys, stolen credentials.
  • Hacking: cameras, alarms, elevator overrides, security routing, power panels.
  • Gadgets: decoys, EMPs, smoke, breaching tools, signal jammers, zip/rappel kits.
  • Found Tech: rare loot that changes how you play (temporary cloak, portable bypass, heat dampener).

Audio/Music Implementation Notes

  • Clarity First: state changes must be unmistakable (players feel it instantly).
  • Stability Under Chaos: rapid combat events can’t break transitions.
  • Graceful Degrade: if bar-sync fails, fallback to stingers + timed fades without losing vibe.

Phase 2 Additions (Beyond Hideouts)

  • More Lieutenants: unique abilities per hideout, stronger narrative voice, and tighter counterplay.
  • More Kingpin Mechanics: corruption themes expressed through boss patterns + arena rules.
  • More Extract Rules: unique extraction minigames (locks, hacks, timed routes, multi-stage escapes).
  • More Weapon Families: hideout-themed weapon types + gadget ecosystems.
  • More Origin Chapters: deepen the gang connection, reveal the revenge chain, escalate stakes.

INTERNAL SUCCESS CRITERIA:

  • Players can describe a run as a single dramatic sentence.
  • Aggro / stealth / smart builds all feel viable and distinct.
  • The Lieutenant is feared, fair, and worth hunting.
  • The music makes “flow” unforgettable.
  • Hideouts feel radically different through traversal + extracts + threats.
