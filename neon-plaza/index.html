Pitch — Page 1 of 2 (Internal)

ELEVATOR PITCH: AKANE’s tempo-driven flow meets Zero Sievert’s extraction tension — set in 1988 neon NYC.

Chain kills into combo-accelerating music, push HEAT to the brink, outsmart a roaming LIEUTENANT, kill the KINGPIN, and extract with rare tech.

Core Identity: absurdly fun to play as a whole — momentum, pressure, and mastery.

The Player Fantasy

  • Combat-forward: lethal top-down action with fast restarts and constant forward motion.
  • Stealth optional: play it as a heist where combat is a consequence — or go loud and ride the soundtrack.
  • Smart play wins: locks, keys, hacking, gadgets, and improvisation create advantages.
  • Extraction stakes: take the loot, survive the escape, tell the story.

Core Loop (20–30 Minutes)

  1. Infiltrate a procedural hideout (floor-by-floor).
  2. Loot + MacGyver (keys, hacks, gadgets, shortcuts).
  3. Build Combo by chaining kills → music intensifies → loot quantity spikes.
  4. Heat Escalates as stealth breaks → security response grows.
  5. Lieutenant Hunts (optional mini-boss; kill for reward + Heat relief).
  6. Kill the Kingpin in a handcrafted fight.
  7. Extract at one of two exits — survive until the sequence completes.

Signature Tension: Heat + Lieutenant

  • HEAT rises as a consequence of losing stealth (detection, alarms, loud play).
  • LIEUTENANT is the roaming predator that turns runs into stories (tracks by sound and sight).
  • CRITICAL RULE: if the Lieutenant is alive and HEAT > 50%, they will join the boss fight when the Kingpin hits 50% health.
  • Meaningful choice: hunt the Lieutenant now, or accept the guaranteed nightmare merge later.

Modes (Formalized)

Progression Split: ORIGIN unlocks CONTENT. REVENGE unlocks ENHANCEMENTS.

Origin is the first contact and the narrative backbone. Revenge is the extraction mastery loop where you perfect builds, improve gear, and chase achievements.

ORIGIN MODE (CONTENT)

  • Linear boss hunt: training + narrative + mastery.
  • Completing Origin unlocks the next hideout.
  • Progressive rewards inside Origin (see Page 4).
  • Heat Model: always rises; stealth reduces the rise rate.

REVENGE MODE (ENHANCEMENTS)

  • Extraction roguelite: loot → boss → extract.
  • Unlocked on your first Origin attempt for that hideout (even if you fail).
  • Rewards: ammo tiers, armor tiers, perks, cosmetics, mastery goals.
  • Heat Model: Heat can drop only during Stealth (full stealth fast, soft stealth slow).

Tech — Page 1 of 2 (State + Heat)

State + Intensity (Canonical Rules)

Layer Values Rules (Exact)
STATE Stealth / Skirmish / Combat / Boss Stealth: undetected (default)
Skirmish: detected OR alarms triggered; you’re not stealth but not combo-ing
Combat: entering combo
Boss: boss fights only
INTENSITY Low / Medium / High Heat Heat rises as a consequence of losing stealth (detection/alarms/loud play).
Heat can drop only during Stealth state (Revenge Mode).
BOSS INTENSITY Boss health phases Boss intensity follows boss health (not Heat).

Soft Stealth vs Full Stealth

Full Stealth: not detected, low detectability → Heat drops rapidly.

Soft Stealth: not detected but being detectable (risk is high) → Heat drops slowly.

Player clarity: you can track which stealth tier you’re in by watching the Heat drop rate.

Heat Behavior (Origin vs Revenge)

REVENGE MODE

  • Heat can decrease only during Stealth state.
  • Full stealth: rapid decrease.
  • Soft stealth: slow decrease.
  • Relief valve: killing Lieutenant can reduce Heat, but usually raises it first (balance carefully).

ORIGIN MODE

  • Heat only rises (unless you kill the Lieutenant).
  • Stealth reduces rise rate (more time to reach boss).
  • Higher Heat at boss entry = more waves / more chaos.
  • Super fight: Heat > 50% + Lieutenant alive = brutal.

Guaranteed Merge Rule (Boss + Lieutenant)

Guaranteed: if the Lieutenant is alive and Heat > 50%, they will enter the boss fight when the Kingpin hits 50% HP.

Design intent: creates a clear mid-fight escalation that players can anticipate and plan around.

Extraction Rule

After killing the Kingpin: you may extract without clearing remaining enemies.

But: you must survive until the extraction sequence completes.

Progression Split (Formal)

ORIGIN UNLOCKS CONTENT. REVENGE UNLOCKS ENHANCEMENTS.

  • Origin: gates the campaign and unlocks new hideouts, gear families, enemies, and systems.
  • Revenge: tracks mastery and improves performance (ammo tiers, perks, cosmetics, prestige).

Origin Unlocks (Content, Progressive Rewards)

Origin should feel rewarding even before completion. Players get staged unlocks as they push deeper.

Origin Milestone Unlock Type Example Reward (Per Hideout)
Enter Origin (first attempt) Access unlock Unlock that hideout’s Revenge Mode even if you fail.
Complete 2 floors Signature gadget Unlock that hideout’s signature gadget (new verb).
Kill Lieutenant Signature weapon Unlock that hideout’s signature weapon family.
Kill Kingpin (complete Origin) Content progression Unlock next hideout + major story reveal.

Key best-practice rule: Origin rewards should feel like new toys and new places, not just stat bumps.

Reason: content unlocks motivate exploration and make failure feel like progress.

Phase Structure (Content)

  • Phase 1 (12 months): Office Tower + Hotel.
  • Phase 2 (+12 months): Shopping Center + Casino.
