NEON PLAZA

Design Bible — Page 1 (Pitch + Pillars)

ELEVATOR PITCH: AKANE’s tempo-driven flow meets Zero Sievert’s extraction tension — set in 1988 neon NYC.

Chain kills into combo-accelerating music, push HEAT to the brink, outsmart a roaming LIEUTENANT, kill the KINGPIN, and extract with rare tech.

Core Identity: absurdly fun to play as a whole — momentum, pressure, mastery.

Design Pillars (Non-Negotiable)

1) FUN-FIRST FLOW: the product is how it feels minute-to-minute. Fast restarts. No dead time.
2) HEAT + MUSIC FUSED: state/intensity is the UI. You should hear danger coming.
3) LIEUTENANT STORIES: Lieutenants define runs. Fair. Iconic. Always answerable.
4) OUTSMART / OUTRUN / HIDE: counterplay is always available, regardless of gear.
5) CONTENT VS ENHANCEMENT VS OPERATIONS: Origin unlocks content. Revenge unlocks enhancements. Chips buy run advantages.

The Player Fantasy

  • Combat-forward: lethal top-down action with constant forward motion.
  • Stealth optional: heist-forward runs are viable; combat becomes consequence.
  • Smart play wins: hacking, locks, gadgets, and improvisation create advantages.
  • Extraction stakes: bank loot and chips by surviving the escape.

Core Loop (20–30 Minutes)

  1. Infiltrate procedural hideout floors.
  2. Loot + MacGyver (keys, hacks, gadgets, shortcuts).
  3. Build Combo → music spikes → earn more chips.
  4. Heat escalates as stealth breaks → security response grows.
  5. Lieutenant hunts (kill for reward + banking boost + heat relief).
  6. Kill Kingpin and survive the merge rule if triggered.
  7. Extract — survive until the sequence completes.

Signature Tension: Heat + Lieutenant

  • HEAT rises as a consequence of losing stealth (detection, alarms, loud play).
  • LIEUTENANT is the roaming predator (tracks by sound + sight).
  • MERGE RULE: if Lieutenant is alive and Heat > 50%, they join the boss fight when the Kingpin hits 50% HP (guaranteed).

Hideouts (Corruption Archetypes)

Each hideout has: traversal hook, extracts, landmarks, enemy identity, loot identity, and a Kingpin + Lieutenant duo designed to be deadly alone and terrifying together.

PHASE 1 — OFFICE TOWER (NAKATOMI PLAZA-INSPIRED)

  • Traversal: vents + security shafts + stairwells
  • Landmarks: server room, executive office, helipad
  • Extracts: helipad / subway access
  • Theme: corporate power + exploitation

PHASE 1 — HOTEL

  • Traversal: elevators + service corridors + suites
  • Landmarks: lobby, restaurant, boardroom
  • Extracts: rooftop parachute / street exit
  • Theme: luxury influence + blackmail

PHASE 2 — SHOPPING CENTER

  • Traversal: escalators + backrooms + loading docks
  • Landmarks: jewelry store, fashion house, electronics store
  • Extracts: loading dock / maintenance tunnel
  • Theme: consumer greed + trafficking

PHASE 2 — CASINO (FINAL)

  • Traversal: open pit + ring halls + vault + high roller
  • Landmarks: gambling pit, vault, high roller room
  • Extracts: car garage / rappel exit
  • Theme: vice + money laundering

Kingpins + Lieutenants (Named, Complementary)

Rule: Lieutenants match the Kingpin theme but use a different playstyle. Together they become extra deadly.

OFFICE TOWER

LIEUTENANT: Maeve “Sentry” Hollis — surveillance chief; always watching.

  • Lieutenant: drones + lockdown pulses + camera traps.
  • Kingpin: protocol phases + Heat-based security waves.
  • Merge: route denial + forced patterns.

HOTEL

KINGPIN: Luis “Concierge” Vargas — broker of secrets and favors.

LIEUTENANT: Yuri “Housekeeper” Morozov — silent enforcer who cleans up mistakes.

  • Lieutenant: silence zones + ambush routes + flanks.
  • Kingpin: decoys + suite-to-suite chase phases.
  • Merge: punishes noise while Kingpin controls space.

SHOPPING CENTER

KINGPIN: Vivian “Brand” Choi — turns consumerism into a pipeline.

LIEUTENANT: “Turnstile” Tommy Reyes — controls entrances and crowds.

CASINO

KINGPIN: Sal “The Dealer” Marino — rigs the city like a table.

LIEUTENANT: “Blackjack” Mariana Rojas — fast and ruthless.

  • Lieutenant: rushdowns + pressure pushes + punishes hesitation.
  • Kingpin: “house advantage” hazards + vault locks.
  • Merge: rushdown + safe-zone denial.

Story Spine (Origin Mode)

Loose backstory: the gangs are connected. Each hideout is a different face of corruption in the same city.

  • Origin reveals why you want revenge and how the factions feed into each other.
  • Each duo represents an ecosystem: power + enforcement.

Design Bible — Page 3 (Systems Contract)

State + Intensity (Canonical)

Layer Values Rules (Exact)
STATE Stealth / Skirmish / Combat / Boss Stealth: undetected (default)
Skirmish: detected OR alarms triggered; not stealth but not combo-ing
Combat: entering combo
Boss: boss fights only
INTENSITY Low / Medium / High Heat Heat rises as a consequence of losing stealth.
Heat drops only during Stealth state (Revenge Mode).
BOSS INTENSITY Boss health phases Boss intensity follows boss health (not Heat).

Soft Stealth vs Full Stealth

Full Stealth: not detected, low detectability → Heat drops rapidly.

Soft Stealth: not detected but being detectable → Heat drops slowly.

Player clarity: you track stealth tier by watching Heat drop rate.

Heat Model (Mode Differences)

REVENGE MODE

  • Heat can decrease only in Stealth.
  • Full stealth drops fast; soft stealth drops slow.
  • Killing Lieutenant can reduce Heat (net outcome must be balanced).

ORIGIN MODE

  • Heat only rises.
  • Stealth reduces rise rate (more time to reach boss).
  • Higher Heat at boss entry = more waves / chaos.

Merge Rule (Guaranteed Escalation)

Guaranteed: Lieutenant alive + Heat > 50% → Lieutenant enters boss fight at 50% Kingpin HP.

Design intent: a readable mid-fight escalation that players plan around.

Extraction Rule

After killing the Kingpin: you may extract without clearing remaining enemies.

But: you must survive until the extraction sequence completes.

Counterplay Contract (Always-Available)

  • Outsmart: knowledge, routes, hacks, gadgets.
  • Outrun: traversal and reposition.
  • Hide: break LoS and enter Stealth to cool Heat (Revenge).

Fairness Contract (No Cheap Losses)

  • Players always know why Heat increased.
  • Lieutenant presence is always announced (audio + UI cue).
  • Deaths are explainable: clear damage source and mistake.
  • No invisible, instant, unavoidable failures.

Design Bible — Page 4 (Progression Contract)

The Progression Triangle (Formal)

ORIGIN unlocks CONTENT. REVENGE unlocks ENHANCEMENTS. CHIPS buy OPERATIONS (temporary).

No Overlap Rule: chips never buy permanent unlocks. Perks/ammo tiers are unlocked in Revenge, then purchased with chips to bring into a run.

ORIGIN (CONTENT)

  • Unlocks hideouts (campaign progression).
  • Unlocks signature gadgets and weapon families.
  • Unlocks new enemies, maps, systems, story beats.
  • Always rewarding even when you fail.

REVENGE (ENHANCEMENTS)

  • Unlocks ammo tiers, armor tiers, and global perks.
  • Unlocks mastery cosmetics and prestige goals.
  • Creates build identity and long-term mastery.

CHIPS (OPERATIONS): a global currency themed to the Casino endgame. Earned during raids, banked on extract (partial on death), spent at the Fence on temporary run advantages.

Origin Unlocks (Progressive)

Origin Milestone Unlock Type Example Reward (Per Hideout)
Enter Origin (first attempt) Mode access Unlock that hideout’s Revenge Mode even if you fail.
Complete 2 floors Signature gadget Unlock that hideout’s signature gadget (new verb).
Kill Lieutenant Signature weapon family Unlock that hideout’s signature weapon family.
Kill Kingpin (complete Origin) Campaign progression Unlock next hideout + major story reveal.

Revenge Unlocks (Meta Tracking)

Revenge Milestone Unlock Type Example Reward
Extract 5 times (per hideout) Global perk Unlock a global perk themed to that hideout’s corruption.
Hit 40 combo Ammo tier unlock Unlock next-tier ammo for a weapon family (then purchase with chips).
Kill 1,000 enemies Cosmetic mastery Unlock Kingpin skin / weapon skin / badge (prestige).

Design Bible — Page 5 (Economy + Chips)

Chips (Global Currency)

  • Earned in-run: combo paydays, Heat risk bonuses, contracts, landmarks.
  • Banked: full on extract; partial on death based on floor progress.
  • Spent: at the Fence between raids on temporary advantages (consumables + services).
  • Never buys permanent unlocks.

Combo → Chips (Core Reward Logic)

Combo increases chip income (paycheck fantasy). Heat increases chip yield but raises danger (risk multiplier).

Stealth support: combo cooldown timer is more forgiving in stealth, so stealth players can still cash out through smart chains.

Banking Rules (Death Cashout)

On death: bank a % of earned chips based on floors completed.

  • 0 floors = 10%
  • 1 floor = 20%
  • 2 floors = 30%
  • 3 floors = 40%
  • 4+ floors = 50% (cap before modifiers)

Lieutenant bonus: killing the Lieutenant guarantees minimum banking protection:

  • If bank % would be below 50% → raise to 50%.
  • If bank % would be above 50% → raise to 80%.
  • Bank cap on death: 80% (prevents intentional “death cashouts”).

The Fence (Rotating Store)

Fence selection rotates each run. Inventory is drawn from a pool of what you’ve unlocked.

Guarantee rule: always offers at least one stealth tool, one combat tool, one survival tool, one “panic” option, and one high-risk/high-reward option.

Fence Inventory Categories (What Chips Buy)

CONSUMABLES (ONE-RUN)

  • Medkits, bandages, trauma kits
  • Smoke grenades, flashbangs
  • Decoy devices
  • Door wedges / lockpick sets
  • Portable jammers
  • Breach charges

SERVICES (ONE-RUN)

  • Intel: reveal 1 landmark room
  • Intel: reveal 1 extract
  • Insurance slot: protect 1 item
  • Bribe: delay security response slightly
  • Ammo/armor: purchase tier you’ve unlocked

Pity Gift System (“The City Takes Pity on You”)

Trigger: die 3 runs in a row.

Effect: before the next run, the city grants a high-tier temporary gift for that run (not permanent unlock).

  • Can include a powerful weapon/gear/gadget preview.
  • Must always materially improve survivability or control.
  • Cannot be a permanent unlock.

Purpose: helps weaker players recover, previews future unlocks, and reduces frustration without breaking the progression contract.

Design Bible — Page 6 (Plan + Tuning Targets)

Phase Structure

  • Phase 1 (12 months): Office Tower + Hotel.
  • Phase 2 (+12 months): Shopping Center + Casino (final).

Timeline (Clean)

PHASE 1 (MONTHS 1–12)

  • M1–4: prove combat + audio/Heat + lieutenant + extraction loop
  • M5: procgen quality gates + landmarks
  • M6–7: Office Tower complete
  • M8–9: Hotel complete
  • M10: merge rule tuning + fairness telegraphs
  • M11–12: Origin pacing + progression staging + polish

PHASE 2 (MONTHS 13–24)

  • M13–18: Shopping Center + system deepening
  • M19–24: Casino + vault sequences + extract upgrades
  • Ongoing: new gadgets (verbs), perks, cosmetics, balancing

Tuning Targets (Early Draft — Adjust After Playtests)

Metric Target Why It Matters
Average run length 20–30 minutes Fast loop supports “one more run” flow.
Extraction success rate (overall) ~35–55% (varies by skill) Too low = frustration; too high = no tension.
Heat > 50% + Lieutenant alive endgame “Expert-only” survival Nightmare merge should be feared but fair.
Fence purchase frequency 1–3 items per run Ensures chips matter without inflation.
Pity gift frequency Rare but noticeable Safety net without encouraging intentional failure.

Quality Contract (Internal Success Criteria)

  • Players describe runs as single dramatic sentences.
  • Deaths are explainable; Heat feels earned; Lieutenants feel fair.
  • Origin always rewards forward progress (even when you fail).
  • Revenge rewards mastery without gating content.
  • Chips create satisfying “payday” moments and meaningful pre-raid decisions.
  • “The city takes pity on you” makes bad players recover and keeps them playing.
