NEON PLAZA — Stage 1 Pitch (Revised)

12-MONTH SCOPE — 2 HIDEOUTS

Elevator Pitch: AKANE's tempo-driven combat meets Zero Sievert's extraction tension. Raid yakuza towers in 1988 NYC, chain kills into combo-accelerating music, hunt roaming bosses under Heat pressure, extract with rare tech. Dark new wave synths over jungle breakbeats.

Setting: New York City, 1988

Berlin Wall crumbling. Japan's bubble economy inflating Manhattan. Yakuza fronts occupy skyscrapers. Rain-slicked neon streets reflect pink and cyan.

Visual: Blade Runner futurism meets Akira kinetic violence. Deep blacks + vibrant magentas/cyans. CRT scan lines. VHS artifacts.

Audio: Dark synth (Depeche Mode, New Order) layered over jungle breakbeats (120-180 BPM state-based).

Core Mode Philosophy

REVENGE MODE (Primary Loop): Extraction roguelite. Extract to keep persistent upgrades. Die = lose run loot only. Heat = ebb/flow alert system (action-based). Mini-boss hunts you. Boss guards extract. Dual difficulty: Standard (archetype loadouts) + Hardcore (no restrictions, cranked difficulty).

ORIGIN MODE (Narrative/Tutorial): Linear boss hunt. Fast respawn, permadeath. Heat = pressure clock (passive rise). Victory unlocks hideouts, loadouts, achievements. Skill mastery + power fantasy replay. Fixed loadouts force playstyle variety.

Core Loop (20-30min runs)

FLOW: Chain kills → Combo drives music energy + drop rates (more loot, not better tier)

HUNT: Mini-boss roams, hunts via audio/vision/timer. High-risk kill = high-tier equipment + Heat reduction

LOOT: Quality tied to floor depth/enemy tier. Combo increases drop RATES (quantity bonus)

HEAT: Origin = passive rise (time pressure). Revenge = action-based (consequence pressure)

EXTRACT: Boss guards exit. 2 extraction points per hideout (different risk/reward profiles)

Music System (State + Layer Reactive)

5 Songs Per Hideout (Fixed BPM, No Tempo Scaling):

  • Stealth: 120 BPM dark new wave (tense, minimal)
  • Skirmish: 140 BPM industrial techno (1-3 enemies)
  • Combat: 160 BPM breakbeat/glitchcore (4+ enemies, alarms)
  • Combo: 180 BPM racing jungle (high flow state)
  • Extraction: 180 BPM climax (triumphant synth lead)

Layering System (3 Layers × 3 Energy Levels):

  • Layer 1: Drums/bass (always present)
  • Layer 2: Melody (adds at combo 15+)
  • Layer 3: Intensity (adds at combo 30+)
  • Energy: Base → Medium (15+) → High (30+)
  • Smooth bar-synced state transitions. No tempo artifacts.

Boss Themes (1 Per Boss): Signature style. Same song progressively intensifies through 3 health phases. Layering + energy increase as boss health drops.

Production: 5 songs × 3 layers × 3 energy = 45 stems per hideout + 1 boss × 3 layers × 3 energy = 9 stems. Total: 54 stems × 2 hideouts = 108 audio assets.

Heat System (Dual Implementation)

Origin Mode (Pressure Clock)

  • Rises passively +5% per 60s
  • Rate slows during stealth
  • Rate accelerates during combat
  • Only goes UP (no reduction)
  • Boss difficulty scales to Heat at encounter:
  • 0-30% = Normal
  • 30-60% = Hard
  • 60%+ = Nightmare
  • Forces momentum, rewards speed

Revenge Mode (Ebb & Flow Alert)

  • Rises from actions: Gunfire +15%, kills +5%, alarms +25%, silenced/melee +1%
  • Falls during stealth: -5% per 15s undetected
  • High Heat consequences:
  • 30%+ = Routes blocked (1-2 paths closed)
  • 60%+ = Elite reinforcements every 30s
  • High Heat = Mini-boss audio detection range doubles
  • 90%+ at extraction = Boss brings friends (lieutenants + mini-boss if alive)
  • Speed/combat vs. Stealth choice

Mini-Boss Hunter (Revenge Only)

Spawns: Heat 40%. Tracks via: Audio (gunfire priority) → Vision (search last-known) → Timer (patrol)

Fight Profile: Risky, resource-intensive skill check. High health (burns 2+ magazines OR sustained melee). Pattern-based, dodge-heavy. New/undergeared players should RUN.

Killable: Permanently (this run only). Strategic decision: Fight early = easier late-game.

Rewards:

  • High-tier equipment drop (guaranteed random: silenced SMG, armor-piercing sniper, advanced armor, tactical grenades)
  • -20% Heat reduction (immediate relief)
  • 50 unlock currency (meta-progression boost)

Boss & Extraction

Boss Guards Extract: Must defeat to escape. 2 extraction choices per hideout with different risk/reward.

Boss Fight: 3 health phases (100% → 66% → 33% → 0%). Unique theme song intensifies as health drops. Changes tactics based on arena (aggressive in tight spaces, ranged in open). Optional location-specific mechanics if time permits.

High Heat Extraction: Boss brings friends (lieutenants + mini-boss if alive).

Extraction Examples:

  • Nakatomi: Helipad (roof, exposed, fast) vs. Sewer Tunnel (underground, tight, slow)
  • Casino: Rappel (penthouse descent, risky) vs. Limousine (garage chase, safe)

Fog of War & Stealth

Vision System (Zero Sievert-Inspired): 150° cone, 15m base range. Darkened (not black) beyond cone. Audio reveals: Gunfire/footsteps/alarms pulse 2s outline outside cone (directional awareness).

Equipment Modifiers (Revenge Unlocks):

  • Scopes: Zoom mode (30m range, 60° tunnel vision, -30% movement while aiming)
  • Flashlight: 180° cone, 20m range, alerts enemies in beam (tactical risk/reward)
  • Night Vision: 360° vision, 12m range, green filter (rare drop, high value)
  • Origin Mode: Base vision only (no equipment modifiers)

Audio Detection (Core Stealth): Silenced weapons + melee = minimal Heat (+1%). Loud weapons = Heat spike (+15%). Mini-boss tracks gunfire (15-20m radius). Stealth runs = harder to find.

Procgen (Rule-Based Modular)

Modular Room Chunks: Entrance, corridor, combat arena, treasure, exit. Assembly rules: Combat → Exploration → Combat. Template pool: 10 entrance × 20 corridor × 15 arena = 3,000 combinations.

Quality Gates: 2-3 traversal routes per floor. Minimum 2 rooms between anchors. Boss zones: 3-5 key rooms (~40% of floor), procedurally placed.

NEON PLAZA — Progression & Timeline

12-MONTH SCOPE — SYSTEMS & CONTENT

Origin Mode Loadouts (Achievement-Unlocked)

5 Fixed Loadouts: Shared unlocks across both modes. Forces playstyle variety in Origin, teaches mechanics.

1. NINJA (Default)

  • Weapons: Katana, smoke grenades, throwing stars
  • Perk: "Silent Step" (footsteps 50% quieter)
  • Unlock: Available from start

2. HITMAN

  • Weapons: Silenced pistol, sniper rifle, knife
  • Perk: "Headhunter" (headshot damage +25%)
  • Unlock: Achievement - "Complete Origin without alarms"

3. OPERATOR

  • Weapons: SMG, standard armor, frag grenades (no melee)
  • Perk: "Tactician" (reload while sprinting)
  • Unlock: Achievement - "Complete hideout in under 15 minutes"

4. BRUTE

  • Weapons: Shotgun, heavy armor, baseball bat (katana range, 50% damage)
  • Perk: "Juggernaut" (armor absorbs 1 extra hit)
  • Unlock: Achievement - "Kill 50 enemies with melee"

5. CUSTOM (Final Unlock)

  • Build loadouts like Revenge Standard mode
  • Perk: Player choice from unlocked pool
  • Unlock: Achievement - "Complete all hideouts + discover 10 secrets"

Achievement System (All 3 Types)

Challenge-Based: Speedrun times, no-damage runs, stealth completions, combo milestones

Progress-Based: Complete hideouts, kill bosses, reach floor milestones, extract X times

Discovery-Based: Find secrets, collect lore items, explore hidden areas, discover Easter eggs

Purpose: Increase replayability, give players reasons to return beyond Revenge grinding

Revenge Mode Progression

2 Difficulty Modes:

  • STANDARD: Archetype loadouts (inspired by Origin's 4 core classes). Balanced difficulty. Learn mechanics.
  • HARDCORE: No restrictions. Build any loadout. Difficulty cranked. Elite unlock currency rewards.

15-Tier Meta-Unlocks (Launch: 8 Tiers, Expand Post-Launch):

  • Armor I/II/III (damage reduction tiers)
  • Stamina +20%/+40%/+60% (melee charge capacity)
  • Reload Speed, Movement Speed, Ammo Capacity
  • Health Regen, Dash Count
  • Fog Equipment: Scopes, Flashlight, Night Vision (rare)

Pity System: Death streak improves starting loadout odds. Rare weapon spawn chance increases with consecutive deaths.

Combo Bonus: Higher combo = higher drop RATES (quantity), not quality (tier). Loot quality tied to floor depth/enemy tier.
