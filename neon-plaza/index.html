NEON PLAZA – Pitch (Launch)

PHASE 1: LAUNCH – Months 1-6

Elevator Pitch: A synth-jungle fusion roguelite raiding yakuza towers in late-80s NYC. Chain kills into tempo-accelerating combos, hunt roaming kingpins under constant Heat pressure, extract with rare tech. AKANE's flow meets Zero Sievert's extraction tension—dark new wave synths over videogame jungle breakbeats.

Setting: New York City, 1988

Berlin Wall crumbling. Japan's tech bubble inflating Manhattan real estate. Yakuza fronts occupy skyscrapers. Rain-slicked streets reflect pink and cyan neon.

Visual: Blade Runner's dark futurism + 80s color saturation. Deep blacks, vibrant magentas/cyans. Akira's kinetic violence. CRT scan lines. VHS artifacts.

Audio: Dark synth (Depeche Mode, New Order) + 90s jungle breakbeats (120-180 BPM tempo scaling). Cold wave bass under Amen breaks.

Dual Mode Philosophy

ORIGIN MODE (Narrative/Unlock): Linear path to kingpin. Stealth reduces Heat. Boss hunt via clues. Death = end. Victory unlocks new hideouts, characters, weapons, areas. Take Revenge upgrades into Origin for power fantasy. Fast respawn, permadeath.

REVENGE MODE (Extraction/Power): Loot-focused runs. Extract to keep persistent upgrades (armor tier, stamina, reload speed). Staying longer = higher risk/reward. Killing kingpin resets Heat for extraction push. Hard mode with vertical progression.

Core Loop (30-90s per floor)

FLOW (Combat): Burst through rooms, chain kills, build tempo. Music accelerates 120→180 BPM with combo. Combo affects drop quality.

HUNT (Origin): Find 3 live clues (expire after 3 floors) to reveal kingpin's floor. Audio cues when on boss floor. Stealth lowers Heat.

LOOT (Revenge): Break flow to search. Quality scales with combo. Extract = keep everything.

HEAT (Pressure): Rises +10% per 30s, falls during stealth. Higher Heat = more reinforcements. Balancing act: speed vs thoroughness.

TRAVERSE: Elevators (fast/loud), stairs (quiet), vents (panic escapes). Multi-path floors.

Launch Hideout: Nakatomi Tower

  • 7 Floors: Lobby → Offices ×3 → Security → Executive → Roof (Helipad extraction)
  • Boss: Kingpin roams Server/Executive/Helipad zones (~40% of zone). Audio telegraphs location when on same floor.
  • Gang: Salaryman Yakuza (corporate fronts, tech smuggling)
  • Enemies: Katana enforcers, pistol gunners, shotgun rushers, SMG elites

Music System (Tempo + Layering)

  • Foundation: Starts 120 BPM (tense, moody). Combo drives tempo: 10 kills = 130 BPM, 25 kills = 160 BPM, max combo = 180 BPM
  • Vertical Layers: Drums/bass always present. Combo 10+ adds cold wave melody. Combo 25+ adds Reese bass intensity.
  • Beat-synced: Transitions on bar boundaries. Combo milestones trigger "stings" on-beat.
  • Stingers: Alarm/boss reveal/extraction fire on beat
  • Decay: Lose combo = tempo/layers strip = vulnerable feeling

Progression (Horizontal + Vertical)

Origin Unlocks (Horizontal): Beat Nakatomi → Casino. Beat Casino → Dept Store. Beat Store → Hotel. Linear sequence. Each unlocks new gangs, enemies, weapons, characters, area mechanics.

Revenge Unlocks (Vertical): Extract successfully → persistent upgrades (armor tier, max stamina, reload speed, movement speed, ammo capacity). 15 total tiers. Start with 3 unlocks, expand to 15 by Month 6.

NEON PLAZA – Tech Core (Launch)

PHASE 1: LAUNCH – Months 1-6

Philosophy: Playable Fast → Polish Deep

Goal: Core loop functional in 8 weeks. Music reactive. Boss hunt works. Origin/Revenge modes complete. 6 months = full launch-ready game.

Flow Combat

Pacing: Tempo-driven kill cadence. 120 BPM start = methodical. 180 BPM max = frantic. AKANE weapon stamina forces variety.

Combo System: 4s decay timer. Drives tempo acceleration, music layers, drop quality, visual juice.

Drop Quality: Combo 0-9 = common loot. Combo 10-24 = uncommon. Combo 25+ = rare/elite drops. Incentivizes flow maintenance.

Weapons (Launch): Katana, bat, knife (stamina-based) | Pistol, shotgun, SMG, sniper (ammo-based). Clear telegraphs. TTK: 1-3 hits (basic), 5-7 hits (elite).

Death (Origin): Fast respawn (<2s). Instant restart at hideout entrance. Permadeath = lose run progress.

Tension Systems

Fog-of-War: 120° vision cone. Sound reveals (footsteps/gunfire pulse outline 1-2s). No persistent reveals. Forces tactical movement.

Alarms: Enemy must reach button. Shoot enemy before activation = prevented. Triggered = locks 1-2 routes + reinforcements spawn after 30s delay.

Stealth = Heat Reduction: Staying undetected lowers Heat -5% per 15s. Breaking stealth stops reduction. Enables strategic slow play.

Intel System (Origin Mode)

Clue Types: Camera footage, radio chatter, guard intel, environmental tells. Each floor has 1-2 clues (randomized spawn).

Clue Lifespan: Clues expire after 3 floors. Must maintain "3 live clues" to reveal boss floor. Forces upward momentum.

Boss Floor Reveal: 3 live clues → minimap shows which floor (Server/Exec/Helipad for Nakatomi). Doesn't show exact room.

Boss Audio Cues: Distinct sound (radio static, heavy footsteps, environmental tells) when on same floor as kingpin. Directional. Helps narrow search without hand-holding.

Heat Escalation

Meter: Starts 0%. Rises +10% per 30s during combat/exploration. Falls -5% per 15s during stealth (undetected). Visual: screen CRT distortion, red/cyan pulse.

Thresholds:

  • 30% = Reinforcements every 60s (2-3 enemies)
  • 60% = Reinforcements every 45s (3-4 enemies, 1 elite chance)
  • 90% = Reinforcements every 30s (4-5 enemies, guaranteed elite)

Kingpin Kill (Revenge): Resets Heat to 0%. Creates relief valve for extraction push.

Anti-camping: Stay on cleared floor >90s without vertical movement = bonus wave (5+ enemies).

Procgen (Hand-Picked Templates)

Spine: Nakatomi 7-floor fixed sequence. Each floor type has 8-12 hand-authored templates.

Template Selection: Designer curates "best playing" templates. Procedural assembly from curated pool. Guarantees quality over pure randomness.

Launch Pool: 60-80 total templates across 7 floor types. ~10 per type. Enough variety for 20+ runs without staleness.

Traversal: Elevator (all floors, 3s ride, loud alarm when used), Stairs (adjacent floors only, quiet), Vents (skip 1 floor up, one-way, emergency only).

Anchors: Security hub, vault, armory placed within appropriate floors (Security/Executive). 2-3 routes between anchors per floor.

Boss Hunt (Zero Sievert-Inspired)

Roaming AI: Kingpin patrols ~40% of assigned zone. Moves between 3-5 key rooms. Doesn't hide in corners. Engages enemies (reinforcements) if encountered.

Audio Presence: When player on same floor: Periodic radio chatter (every 20-30s), distinct footstep pattern, environmental reactions (guards saluting, doors opening).

Combat: 3 health phases. Phase 1 (100-66%) = standard AI. Phase 2 (66-33%) = calls reinforcements mid-fight. Phase 3 (33-0%) = desperate, faster attacks, attempts escape to heal.

Anti-stall: If player hasn't found kingpin in 8 minutes = boss auto-reveals on minimap + intense reinforcement wave. Prevents indefinite searching.

Extraction (Revenge Mode Only)

Trigger: Kill kingpin → Heat resets to 0% → 90s timer starts → reach extraction point (Nakatomi = Helipad on Roof).

Finale (Launch): Timed Holdout. Defend helipad for 60s. Waves every 15s (scaling intensity). Final wave = elite squad + mini-boss.

Success: Survive → extraction → keep all loot + earn unlock currency. Die → lose loot, no unlock progress.

Origin Mode: No extraction. Kill kingpin → loot tally → immediate end → Origin unlock (next hideout/character).

Music (Tempo + Vertical Layers)

FMOD Setup:

  • Parameter 1: Combo (0=120 BPM, 10=130, 25=160, 50=180). Tempo scales in real-time.
  • Parameter 2: Layers (0-9=drums/bass only, 10-24=+melody, 25+=+intensity bass)
  • Transitions: Queued to next bar boundary (every 4 beats). Smooth, no jarring cuts.
  • Stingers: Alarm/boss reveal/extraction play on-beat, override queue momentarily.
  • Decay: Lose combo = tempo/layers strip over 2-3 bars. Gradual tension loss.

Defer: Horizontal resequencing (stealth/combat states) to Month 7-8.

Build Philosophy: Systems First, Content Parallel

Core Approach: Establish foundation systems (combat, music, Heat) early. Run content pipeline (templates, assets) in parallel. Integrate continuously. Iterate based on daily playtests.

    Goal: 60-80 hand-authored templates by Week 18. 10-12 per floor type.

    Testing & Balance Workflow

    Per-Update Playtest Cycle:

    • Week 1-2 (Foundation): Internal only. Test new hideout layout/flow
    • Week 3-4 (Mechanics): Team playtests. Test new mechanics integration with existing content
    • Week 5-6 (Polish): External playtest. 10-15 testers. Fresh hideout + retroactive mechanics on old hideouts
    • Week 7-8 (Balance): Iteration based on feedback. Tune new mechanics, fix integration bugs

    Retroactive Integration Testing:

    • Each new mechanic: Test on ALL existing hideouts. Ensure thematic fit (slots, decoys, civilians)
    • Balance check: New mechanics shouldn't trivialize Phase 1 content. Tune if broken
    • Example: Fortune slots in Nakatomi. Does +20% damage break combat tuning? Adjust if needed

    Metrics to Track (Expansion):

    • Average run times: Casino (12-18min), Dept Store (18-25min), Hotel (20-30min). Tune if outliers
    • Civilian kill rates: Track accidental vs intentional. Tune Heat penalty if >50% accidental
    • Decoy false positive rate: Track decoy shoots. Tune visual tells if >30% false positives
    • Chip economy: Track chip earn/spend rates. Adjust elite drop amounts if shop unusable
    • Extraction completion rates: Track deaths during finale. Tune difficulty if <40% success
