Pitch — Page 1 of 2 (Internal)

ELEVATOR PITCH: AKANE’s tempo-driven flow meets Zero Sievert’s extraction tension — set in 1988 neon NYC.

Chain kills into combo-accelerating music, push HEAT to the brink, outsmart a roaming LIEUTENANT, kill the KINGPIN, and extract with rare tech.

Core Identity: it’s absurdly fun to play — moment-to-moment flow is the product.

The Player Fantasy

  • Combat-forward: lethal top-down action with instant restart speed and constant forward motion.
  • Stealth optional: play it as a heist where combat is a consequence — or go loud and farm momentum.
  • Smart play wins: locks, keys, hacking, gadgets, and MacGyver improvisation open new routes and solve problems.
  • Extraction stakes: every run is a push-your-luck story — take the loot, survive the escape.

Core Loop (20–30 Minutes)

  1. Infiltrate a procedural hideout (floor-by-floor).
  2. Loot + MacGyver (keys, hacks, gadgets, route shortcuts).
  3. Build Combo by chaining kills → music intensifies → loot quantity spikes.
  4. Heat Escalates as stealth breaks → security response grows.
  5. Lieutenant Hunts (optional mini-boss; kill for reward + Heat relief).
  6. Kill the Kingpin in a handcrafted fight.
  7. Extract at one of two exits — survive until the sequence completes.

Signature Tension: Heat + Lieutenant

  • HEAT rises as a consequence of losing stealth (detection, alarms, loud play).
  • LIEUTENANT is the roaming predator that turns runs into stories (tracks by sound and sight).
  • CRITICAL RULE: if the Lieutenant is alive and HEAT > 50%, they can join the final fight / extraction chaos alongside the Kingpin.
  • Strategic choice: hunt the Lieutenant now, or risk the nightmare ending later.

Mode Structure (First Contact = Origin)

ORIGIN MODE (First Contact)

  • Linear boss hunt: training + narrative + mastery.
  • Completing a hideout’s Origin unlocks that hideout’s Revenge Mode.
  • Meta Gains: new weapon/equipment types, enemies, maps.
  • Heat Model: always rises; stealth reduces the rate of increase.

REVENGE MODE (Extraction Roguelite)

  • Extraction loop: loot → boss → extract.
  • Meta Gains: better ammo, armor, perks, long-term power.
  • Heat Model: can be reduced by entering stealth over time (cost = TIME).
  • First successful extraction unlocks custom loadouts for Origin.

Why this works: Origin teaches the rules and story. Revenge delivers the heist fantasy and stakes. Both feed progression without flattening skill expression.

Audio + Heat + State Model (Your Core Framework)

This is the backbone of the entire experience: state describes situation; intensity describes Heat tier.

Layer Values Triggers Player Read
STATE Stealth / Skirmish / Combat / Boss Stealth = default
Skirmish = detected OR alarms triggered
Combat = entering combo
Boss = boss fight only 		“What’s happening right now?”
INTENSITY Low Heat / Medium Heat / High Heat Heat tier rises as stealth breaks and time passes under pressure.
In Revenge: stealth reduces Heat over time (cost = time).
In Origin: Heat always rises; stealth reduces rise rate. 		“How bad is it getting?”
BOSS INTENSITY Boss Health Phases Boss intensity follows boss health (not Heat). “How close is victory?”

Environmental Feedback Contract:

  • Heat increases must be visible and audible: flashing lights, alarms, PA announcements, security doors.
  • State changes must be musically obvious: stealth is restrained, combat is aggressive, combo is triumphant.
  • Lieutenant presence has its own signature cue (audio motif + UI indicator).

Boss Difficulty Scaling (Heat as “Consequences”)

  • Origin Mode: higher Heat at boss = more waves / reinforcements.
  • Revenge Mode: Heat affects security response, Lieutenant behavior, and extraction difficulty.
  • Super Fight Condition: Heat > 50% + Lieutenant alive = “experienced players only” endgame scenario.

Fairness Contract (Best-Practice Guardrails)

The game is lethal and high-stakes, but never arbitrary. Every loss must be explainable.

What Players Must Always Know

  • Why Heat increased (detected / alarm / loud action).
  • Whether the Lieutenant is active and where the danger is coming from.
  • How close the extraction sequence is to completion.
  • Why they died (clear damage source and failure point).

What Players Must Always Have

  • At least one viable route forward (never “dead runs”).
  • At least one counterplay tool for the Lieutenant (hide, break LoS, decoy, hack).
  • A meaningful decision under pressure (hunt vs flee vs cool down).
  • A fast restart that respects their time.

Extraction Rule (Important)

After killing the Kingpin: the player can extract without clearing remaining enemies.

But: they must survive until extraction sequence completes.

Result: extraction becomes a cinematic escape, not a cleanup chore.

Lieutenant Design Framework (Make Them Iconic)

Each Lieutenant is as important as each Kingpin. They must be recognizable, fair, and counterplay-rich.

Requirement Definition Implementation Notes
Signature Cue Player instantly knows “it’s them.” Audio motif + UI indicator + unique silhouette.
Independent Threat Lieutenant is deadly alone. Unique pursuit pattern + special ability tied to hideout theme.
Counterplay Vocabulary At least 3 reliable ways to respond. Break LoS, decoys, hacks, traps, vent routes, silence zones, etc.
Theme Pairing Matches Kingpin’s corruption style. Same aesthetic + narrative, different playstyle.
Synergy Combo Together they’re extra deadly. Lieutenant covers the Kingpin’s weakness (rushdown + zoning, silence + drones, etc.).
No Cheap Losses Players understand why they lost. Telegraph abilities; avoid invisible, instant, unavoidable hits.

Hard Best-Practice Rule: Every Lieutenant ability must be answered by at least one of:

  • Skill response (movement, positioning, aim)
  • Stealth response (hiding, LoS breaks, silence, reroutes)
  • Smart response (hack, gadget, trap, decoy)

Procedural Generation: Quality Gates (Best Practices)

Procedural content must feel authored. The goal is perceived variety, not combinatorics.

Validation Gates (Must Pass)

  • At least 2 viable routes forward.
  • No softlocks: keys/hacks have redundancy.
  • Extraction is always reachable.
  • Combat spaces are readable (no spawn traps, no blind instant deaths).
  • Vertical transitions cannot create stuck AI or broken LoS.

Variety Levers (Must Include)

  • Landmarks (server room, vault, lobby, etc.).
  • Rule variants (camera-heavy vs patrol-heavy vs lockdown-heavy).
  • Route archetypes (vents vs hallways vs service routes).
  • Hideout-specific setpieces and “signature moments.”
  • Event rooms (alarm reroute, elevator override, keycard chase).

Progression Structure (Origin ↔ Revenge)

Meta Gains Split: two progression lanes that feed each other without flattening skill.

  • Origin Rewards: unlock new weapons/equipment types, enemies, maps (content breadth).
  • Revenge Rewards: better ammo, better armor, perks (power + comfort).
  • Bridge: first Revenge extraction unlocks custom Origin loadouts (helps skill-challenged players progress).

Accessibility Gotchas (Audio-as-Gameplay)

  • Sound cues must be mirrored with optional visual cues (subtitles, UI pulses).
  • Color-blind safe indicators for Heat tiers and alarms.
  • Difficulty slider should adjust forgiveness (damage, extraction timers, Lieutenant aggression) without breaking core rules.

  • Procgen repetition: landmarks and rule variants must be visible early.

Phase 2 Best-Practice Hurdles (Plan For Them)

  • Scope creep: every new hideout needs 1 traversal hook + 1 rule hook + 3 landmarks — no more.
  • System sprawl: add gadgets as “verbs” (new solutions), not as minor stat variants.
  • Balance drift: ensure loud/stealth/smart remain equally viable as content grows.
  • Two-boss chaos: keep the combined finale short, cinematic, and readable.

INTERNAL SUCCESS CRITERIA:

  • Players describe runs as single dramatic sentences (“I spared Sentry and she joined the helipad fight…”).
  • The game is fun even when you lose (fast restart + clear lessons).
  • Lieutenants are iconic, fair, and worth hunting.
  • Heat and music feel fused; you can hear the city closing in.
  • Each hideout feels radically different from the others.
